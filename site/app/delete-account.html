KIN

Delete your KIN account

In the app

Open KIN, go to Settings, then Delete Account, and confirm. We record your request straight away.

Without the app

Email support@kinship.living from the address you use to sign in to KIN, and say you want your account deleted. We may ask you to confirm it's you before we act on it.

What deletion removes

Your account, your profile and answers, your card, your connections and their messages, and the vouches written for you. Safety reports and the moderation decisions on them, and your messages to support, stay, no longer linked to your account. Backups that still hold your data expire within our backup window.

Deleting your account can't be undone. If you want to come back later, you'll need a new invitation.
